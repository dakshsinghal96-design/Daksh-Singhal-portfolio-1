About the venue

Set on a four-acre island in the middle of Lake Pichola, the Taj Lake Palace is an 18th-century marvel of white marble. Weddings here span candle-lit courtyards, lily-pond terraces and a private jetty arrival by boat — a cinematic entry no other Udaipur venue can match. Our team coordinates royal welcomes, lakeside pheras and rooftop sangeets.
