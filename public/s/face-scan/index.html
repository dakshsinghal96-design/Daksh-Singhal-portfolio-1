AI Tongue Appearance Scan

This scan helps you take a clear, well-lit photograph of the top surface of your tongue, and then tells you whether that photograph is good enough to be useful. It evaluates visible appearance and image quality only.

This is not a medical diagnosis. It does not diagnose disease and does not replace a doctor or dentist. A photograph cannot show every area of the mouth. If anything about your mouth worries you, speak to a dentist or doctor.

How it works

  1. PrepareCheck your lighting, clean the lens, and read a short checklist.
  2. PositionLine your mouth up with the on-screen guide.
  3. CaptureHold still while a few frames are taken.
  4. ReviewLook at the image, see the quality check, and retake if you want.
🔒 Images are processed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is stored after you leave.

Prepare for your scan

A few seconds of setup makes the image much more useful.

Checklist

Clean the camera lens.
Use bright, neutral, indirect lighting.
Remove lipstick or strongly coloured mouth products where possible.
Avoid beauty filters and camera effects.
Keep the phone steady.
Extend the tongue naturally, without straining.

Recent food, drink, or mouthwash can change how the surface looks.

Do not continue if there is active bleeding, severe pain, or a medical emergency. Stop straight away if extending your tongue is painful or uncomfortable, and seek care.

      What this result means

      This result confirms only that an image was captured and selected using technical quality checks such as positioning, focus, lighting, reflections, and movement.

      What this result does not mean

      This result does not assess whether your tongue is healthy or unhealthy. It does not diagnose or rule out an oral or medical condition. A single front-facing image cannot examine the entire mouth.

      Tongue appearance analysis is not enabled in this version.
      🔒 This image is held temporarily in this browser session. It has not been uploaded or permanently saved. The image will be removed when you finish, discard the session, leave the page, or when the session is otherwise cleared.

      When to seek professional care

      This scan cannot diagnose or rule out oral disease. Arrange an assessment with a dentist or doctor if you have a sore, irritation, lump, thick patch, red or white patch, unexplained mouth pain or bleeding, or difficulty chewing, swallowing, speaking, or moving the tongue that lasts for more than two weeks.
