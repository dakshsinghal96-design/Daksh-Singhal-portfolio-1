What is Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the estimated amount of carbon dioxide released because of our everyday activities. Vehicle fuel, petrol, diesel, electricity, LPG, public transport, waste and lifestyle choices all create an environmental impact.

🚗

Transport Impact

Petrol and diesel usage are major contributors to personal and city-level emissions.

⚡

Energy Impact

Electricity consumption creates indirect CO₂ emissions depending on the power source.

🌳

Action Impact

Simple choices like public transport, vehicle maintenance, energy saving and tree plantation can reduce emissions.

Why This Is Important

Carbon awareness helps citizens understand their role in clean air, climate protection, healthier cities and responsible development. This calculator is designed as an easy public education tool for all users.
