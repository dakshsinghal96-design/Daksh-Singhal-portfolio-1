Phase 01 · live readout

Eye behaviour, measured. Not deception.

OCULAR computes per-frame features of the eye — aperture, iris geometry, gaze direction — from a webcam in your browser. The signals correlate with cognitive load. They do not, on their own, identify lies.

— how this is being built —

Trained with the Concealed Information Test.

Not a naïve "truth vs lie" split. Subjects pick a card 1–5 in private; later they're asked "is it 1?", "is it 2?" in random order, answering "no" to each. Only the card that matches their concealed item produces a meaningful cognitive-load signal. Combined with per-subject baselines and subject-level cross-validation.

— ethics statement —

This is not a lie detector.

Eye-movement signals correlate with cognitive load — attention, effort, surprise — but no method using a consumer webcam reliably distinguishes deception from other sources of stress. Anyone presenting tools like this as truth-detection technology is overstating what the science supports.

Cognitive-load estimation has legitimate applications: driver drowsiness, UX research, education, accessibility. The same signals can be weaponised in interrogation, hiring, surveillance, and border control. OCULAR is framed deliberately to discourage that.
