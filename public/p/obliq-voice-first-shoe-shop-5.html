Voice commands.

Talk to the shop. The system understands natural sentences.

"show all"view every shoe
"show running" / "casual" / "boots"filter by category
"on sale" / "deals"only sale items
"under fifty"price filter (any number)
"cheapest first"sort low to high
"most expensive first"sort high to low
"recommend something"random pick
