Heart

☆⌃

Function

The heart is a muscular pump that circulates blood throughout the body via the pulmonary and systemic circulations, delivering oxygen and nutrients and removing waste.

Clinical Notes

  • Coronary artery disease is the most common cause of heart disease.
  • Signs of heart failure include fatigue, edema, and shortness of breath.
  • Monitor ECG, blood pressure, and biomarkers for assessment.
