Voice commands.

Talk to the shop. The system understands natural sentences.
// browse
"show all"view every shoe
"show running"filter to running
"show formal"filter to formal
"show casual" / "boots" / "sandals"filter by category
"cheapest first"sort low to high
"most expensive"sort high to low
// shopping
"add running shoes"add by name
"add product one"add by number (1–6)
"remove boots"remove an item
"clear cart"empty everything
"wishlist running"save for later
// cart & checkout
"open cart" / "show my bag"open the cart drawer
"close cart"close it
"what's the total"speaks total amount
"checkout"open checkout
// system
"hello"greeting
"who are you"about Obliq
"help"this menu
"close"close any modal
"stop"turn mic off
"scroll up / down / top"scroll the page
Tap mic & say "show running"
