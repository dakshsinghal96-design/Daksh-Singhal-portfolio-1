Privacy Center

Visibility and control over your data. Designed with DPDP / GDPR / SOC 2 readiness concepts. Requires legal, security, and expert review before production launch.

What we collect

  • Profile basics (name, age, class, city)
  • Assessment responses
  • Optional portfolio uploads
  • Consent and audit metadata

What we do with it

  • Generate your career guidance
  • Improve our models (with consent)
  • Maintain audit and consent logs
  • Never sell to third parties
Data Retention Note: In a production deployment, assessment data would be retained while the account is active, with incomplete assessments auto-deleted after 90 days. Consent and audit records would be retained for up to 7 years for legal and audit purposes. When backend storage is not configured, data is held only in your browser's local storage.

Focus & Calm Mode

What it is

  • Optional supportive feature
  • Camera used only during the assessment
  • No recording, no storage, no screenshots
  • No biometric identification or face recognition
  • Can be turned off anytime

Boundaries

  • Parent/guardian consent required for minors
  • Optional supportive feature
  • Not a medical or psychological diagnosis
  • No camera data is sent anywhere
  • All processing stays in your browser
Disclaimer: Focus & Calm Mode is a supportive, optional feature. It does not diagnose emotions, stress, anxiety, or mental health conditions.
