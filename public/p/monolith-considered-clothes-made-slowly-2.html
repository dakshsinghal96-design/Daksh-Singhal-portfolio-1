— Our way of working —

Made in small batches, by people we know.

Every piece comes from one of two small factories — fifteen people in Tirupur for jersey, twelve in Ludhiana for knitwear. We visit twice a year, pay above market rate, and produce in batches of 200. If something arrives wrong, we'll repair, replace, or refund it — no time limit.
