1Product Overview

Obliq AI Face Scan is an AI-powered camera-based wellness and appearance scanner, designed as an educational and demonstration tool by the Obliq AI Education Foundation.

In just 30 seconds, the system simulates a wellness analysis using the user's front-facing camera and presents a structured report covering:

  • Wellness signals — Stress level, heart rate, breathing rate, blood pressure estimate, energy level, relaxation score, overall wellness score, and scan quality
  • Visual appearance highlights — Eye & eyebrow health, face structure, and skin appearance (each with a one-line summary plus optional detailed breakdown)
  • A downloadable PDF report branded with the Obliq AI Education Foundation logo and the user's personal details

The product is fully browser-based, requires no app installation, and is optimised for both mobile phones and desktops.

3User Journey — Step by Step

The full experience takes about 90 seconds from landing to downloaded report.

1
Landing Page

When the site opens, the user sees the Obliq AI Face Scan brand header, a hero description, three feature cards, a privacy note, and a medical disclaimer. The primary call-to-action is a single Start Scan button, gated by a consent checkbox: "I consent to local camera-based demo analysis."

User action Tick the consent box, then tap Start Scan.

Friendly nudge

If the user taps Start Scan without ticking consent, the consent box highlights with a red shake animation.

2
Pre-Scan Form

A clean, mobile-friendly form asks for the user's basic details. All three fields are required, with inline validation:

FieldValidation
Full NameMinimum 2 characters, max 60
Phone Number7–15 digits (any standard format with +, spaces, -)
AgeNumber between 13 and 120

User action Fill in Name, Phone Number, Age. Tap Continue to Scan.

Privacy

Personal details are stored only in the user's browser memory and are added to the downloadable PDF report. Nothing is sent to a server.

3
Confirmation Modal

A confirmation pop-up appears asking "Are you sure you want to start the scan?" with a short note that the camera will turn on and a 30-second wellness scan will begin. Two buttons: Yes, start scan (primary) and Not yet (secondary).

User action Tap Yes, start scan.

Mobile UX

On mobile, the modal slides up from the bottom of the screen as a native-feeling action sheet.

4
Face Scan (30 seconds)

The camera permission dialog appears (browser-controlled). Once granted, the user sees:

  • Their live camera feed in a dark video card
  • Cyan corner brackets framing the face
  • A dashed pulsing ring indicating active scanning
  • A horizontal scan-line sweeping up and down across the face
  • A "Live Camera" chip and a blinking red "Scanning" chip in the top-left

The right-hand panel (or below, on phones) shows the Wellness Scan dashboard:

  • A circular SVG countdown timer (30 → 0 seconds)
  • A linear progress bar filling 0 % → 100 %
  • A status line updating in real time
  • A 6-stage activity list: Detecting face position → Checking lighting quality → Analyzing visual signals → Estimating wellness indicators → Reviewing appearance features → Preparing scan report
  • A Cancel Scan button to abort if needed

The scan starts automatically once the camera is ready — no extra tap required.

Note: All scan results are AI-simulated. The product does not perform real biometric measurement.

Download PDF Report

At the bottom of the results screen are four action buttons:

What the PDF contains

  • Branded header — "OBLIQ AI EDUCATION FOUNDATION" tagline + "Face Scan Report" title + timestamp
  • Inline SVG logo in mint / periwinkle / violet brand gradient
  • Scan recipient card — Name, Phone Number, Age
  • Wellness summary grid — all 8 wellness metrics including Stress %
  • Disclaimer stating the report is issued by Obliq AI Education Foundation as a demonstration only
  • Footer — © Obliq AI Education Foundation · timestamp

Filename format: Obliq-FaceScan-{Name}-{Timestamp}.pdf

How the download works

  • Desktop / Android: the PDF downloads directly to the user's Downloads folder.
  • iPhone (Safari): a new tab opens with a "Generating PDF…" spinner. Once ready, the PDF appears inline. The user taps Safari's share button (square with up-arrow) and chooses Save to Files to keep a copy.

A live mint toast shows progress at every step, so the user always knows what's happening.

4Key Features at a Glance

  • Zero installation — runs in any modern browser
  • Mobile-first — every screen is responsive and touch-optimised
  • Accessible — ARIA live regions, focus management, screen-reader announcements, prefers-reduced-motion support
  • Privacy-first — no photos, videos, or biometric data are stored or transmitted
  • Branded report — official PDF with Obliq AI Education Foundation logo, colours, and disclaimer
  • Educational disclaimer — every relevant surface clearly marks the demo as educational, not medical
  • Cinematic aesthetic — dark holographic theme with mint / periwinkle / violet gradients, frosted glass panels, animated background orbs

5Privacy & Data

  • The camera feed is processed entirely on the user's device.
  • No images, video frames, or biometric measurements ever leave the browser.
  • The user's Name, Phone Number, and Age are stored only in browser memory for the current session — they are written into the downloadable PDF and never sent to a server.
  • Closing the browser tab clears all session data.
  • The site is served exclusively over HTTPS with strict security headers (CSP-style frame, content-type, referrer, and permissions policies enforced by Vercel).

7Important Disclaimer

Obliq AI Face Scan is an educational demonstration only.

It does not measure real medical vitals and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, or any medical, legal, hiring, insurance, or official decisions. All wellness and appearance values are AI-simulated for demo and educational purposes and may be inaccurate.

For any real health concerns, users should always consult a qualified healthcare professional.
