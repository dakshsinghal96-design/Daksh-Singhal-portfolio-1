Gesture Control Guide

Control Obliq AI using hand gestures via your webcam.

Privacy: Webcam frames are processed entirely on-device. No video is transmitted, stored, or shared.

Setup

1

Click 'Hand: ON' in the navigation bar.

2

Allow webcam access when prompted.

3

Position your hand 30–60 cm from the camera.

4

Wait for the floating panel to show 'Active'.

5

All gestures plus keyboard fallbacks (C, G, H, ↑↓←→, Esc) are available anytime.
