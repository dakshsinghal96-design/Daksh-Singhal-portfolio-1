Capabilities

Everything between a prompt and production.

One unified layer that handles the unglamorous engineering so your team can focus on the product, not the pipeline.

Smart model routing

Automatically send each request to the fastest, cheapest model that meets your quality bar — switch providers without changing a line.

Continuous evals

Score every response against your own rubric in real time. Catch regressions before your users ever see them.

Built-in guardrails

Block prompt injection, PII leaks and off-policy output with configurable, low-latency safety filters.

End-to-end tracing

Every token, tool call and retry captured in a clean timeline. Debug a bad answer in seconds, not hours.

Cost intelligence

Live spend dashboards, budgets and per-team limits. Know exactly what every feature costs before the invoice.

Edge-fast delivery

Globally distributed runtime with zero cold starts — your AI features feel instant, anywhere in the world.
